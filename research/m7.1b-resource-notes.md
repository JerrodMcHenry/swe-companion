## Corrections to the M7.1B specification

**Think Python 3rd edition chapter numbers are off by one in the spec.**

| Spec said | Actual chapter | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Ch. 10 Lists | Ch. 9 Lists | https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkPython/chap09.html |
| Ch. 11 Dictionaries | Ch. 10 Dictionaries | https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkPython/chap10.html |
| Ch. 12 Tuples | Ch. 11 Tuples | https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkPython/chap11.html |
| Ch. 10 (aliasing) | Ch. 9, sections 9.9–9.11 | https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkPython/chap09.html#objects-and-values |

Chapter 12 is "Text Analysis and Generation". Useful sections: 9.1–9.8 (list basics, methods, sorting), 9.9 Objects and values, 9.10 Aliasing, 9.11 List arguments; 10.1–10.8 (dictionaries; 10.4 counters, 10.8 Memos for caching); 11.1–11.5 (tuples, immutability, assignment, return values, argument packing), 11.6 Zip, 11.7 Comparing and Sorting (uses `key=`).

**Python Wiki TimeComplexity has moved.** https://wiki.python.org/moin/TimeComplexity redirects to https://wiki.python.org/python/language/TimeComplexity.html, which says the page moved to the official docs: https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/time-complexity.html ("Time complexity of operations on built-in types"). That page states its costs are for CPython. Section anchors: `#list`, `#tuple`, `#dict-frozendict`, `#set-frozenset`.

**Mapping Types anchor changed.** `stdtypes.html#mapping-types-dict` no longer exists; use https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#mapping-types-dict-frozendict (Python 3.15 docs).
