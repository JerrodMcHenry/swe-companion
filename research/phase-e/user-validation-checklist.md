# Lightweight user validation (manual, 10–20 job seekers)

Internal. No analytics, accounts or feedback platform: a facilitator observes and takes notes in a spreadsheet. Do not claim SWE Companion improves hiring outcomes until outcome evidence exists.

## Participants (aim for a mix)

| Profile | Target count | Example targets |
|---|---|---|
| New graduates | 3–4 | Google SWE, enterprise graduate program |
| Career switchers | 2–3 | General SWE, data, solutions |
| Experienced engineers | 3–4 | Backend at enterprise, ML system design, frontend at Amazon |
| Engineers affected by layoffs | 2–3 | Any role they are actively applying for |
| Specialties | across the above | Frontend, data, ML, AI, FDE, solutions, quant developer |

Record for each: profile, target role and employer (or employer type), years of experience, date. Use first names or codes only; do not record contact details in the results sheet.

## Session (20–30 minutes, screen share or in person)

Say only: "You have an interview for [their real or chosen target] in a week. Use this site to decide what to prepare." Do not demonstrate the site or explain the navigation.

Tasks (pick 3–4 matching the participant):
1. Find what to focus on for your target employer and role.
2. Find and open the questions you would practise first, and read one answer.
3. Find coding or practical problems relevant to your interview (SQL, JavaScript, design, debugging…).
4. Find one resource you would actually use, and say why.
5. Find advice on a non-technical step you need now (resume, applying, negotiating an offer).
6. Use search for a topic you are worried about.

## Measure (facilitator notes)

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Found relevant material? | Yes / partly / no per task |
| Time to first useful material | Stopwatch, seconds |
| Clicks to first useful material | Count |
| Understood priorities? | Ask: "What would you study first, and why?" |
| Resources save time? | Ask per resource opened: "Would you have found this yourself? Is it worth the time?" |
| Answers useful? | Ask: "Would you say this in an interview? Anything wrong or unclear?" |
| Missing content? | Ask: "What did you expect to find that wasn't here?" |
| Unnecessary complexity? | Note hesitations, back-navigation, confusion; ask what was confusing |

## Closing questions

1. Would you use SWE Companion before your next interview? (yes / maybe / no, and why)
2. What one change would make it more useful?
3. Optional and voluntary: may we contact you later to ask how your interviews went? If yes, record consent and contact details separately from the results, and delete them when no longer needed.

## Afterwards

- Summarize issues by frequency and impact (blocked a task > slowed it > cosmetic).
- Fix only issues seen in more than one session or that blocked a task, consistent with the simplicity requirements.
- Outcome reports (offers, interviews) are anecdotal: record them as such and do not publish claims based on them.
